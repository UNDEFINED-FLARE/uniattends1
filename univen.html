Manage Venues (Admin)

Draw the exact outline of a classroom or hall once. Every lecturer can then select it when creating a session or timetable entry — far more accurate than a simple radius around one pin, and no lecturer has to draw it themselves.

How a check-in gets verified

  • Eight location readings are taken over nine seconds and outliers are thrown away, so one GPS jump cannot decide anything.
  • The boundary test asks how much of the reading’s uncertainty overlaps this outline, not where the pin happened to land.
  • Each student is compared against where the rest of the checked-in class is sitting.
  • The session QR rotates every 30 seconds, so a screenshot sent to a friend outside is already dead.
  • One phone can only check in one student per session.
  • Borderline results are marked present and sent to the lecturer to confirm, never silently refused.
