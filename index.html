How it decides

  1. Every device in the venue records the same few seconds, taken from the lecturer's window on the server's clock.
  2. Each phone reduces its audio to a few hundred spectral landmarks and discards the recording. Audio never leaves the device.
  3. The server compares landmark sets pairwise, keeping only matches whose timing alignment spikes rather than scatters.
  4. Devices that heard the same air form a cluster. The cluster has to contain the lecturer's phone to count as this class.
  5. Landmarks are deleted the moment the verdict exists. Only the verdict and its reasons survive.

This proves a phone was in the room, not who was holding it, so it never marks anyone absent on its own: a device that does not cluster is routed to the lecturer for review.
